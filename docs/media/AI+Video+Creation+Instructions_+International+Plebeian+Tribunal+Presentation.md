# AI Video Creation Instructions: International Plebeian Tribunal Presentation

## Project Overview
Create a comprehensive video presentation for the International Plebeian Tribunal peace initiative that will be embedded in the "View Presentation" section of the website (https://wdjfyscq.manus.space). This presentation should serve as the primary introduction to the organization's mission, goals, and revolutionary approach to global peace advocacy.

## Video Specifications
- **Duration:** 8-12 minutes
- **Format:** MP4, 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Style:** Professional, inspiring, and accessible
- **Tone:** Hopeful, empowering, and revolutionary yet peaceful
- **Target Audience:** Global citizens, peace advocates, potential chapter organizers, and social justice activists

## Visual Style Guidelines

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep blue (#1e3a8a) representing trust and stability
- **Secondary:** Vibrant green (#10b981) representing growth and hope
- **Accent:** Gold (#f59e0b) representing value and achievement
- **Background:** Clean white (#ffffff) with subtle gradients

### Typography
- **Headers:** Bold, modern sans-serif (Montserrat or similar)
- **Body Text:** Clean, readable sans-serif (Open Sans or similar)
- **Emphasis:** Strategic use of color and weight for key points

### Animation Style
- **Smooth Transitions:** Professional, not flashy
- **Data Visualization:** Clean, informative charts and graphs
- **Geographic Elements:** World maps, country highlights, connection lines
- **Organizational Charts:** Clear hierarchy with smooth expand/collapse

### Imagery Requirements
- **Diverse Representation:** People from all continents, ages, backgrounds
- **Peaceful Imagery:** Community gatherings, collaborative work, celebration
- **Technology Integration:** Modern interfaces, clean dashboards, futuristic elements
- **Natural Elements:** Earth imagery, sustainable development, environmental harmony

## Audio Requirements

### Narration
- **Voice:** Professional, warm, inspiring (gender-neutral or alternating voices)
- **Pace:** Measured, allowing for comprehension of complex concepts
- **Tone:** Hopeful and empowering, avoiding doom-and-gloom messaging
- **Language:** Clear, accessible language avoiding jargon

### Background Music
- **Style:** Uplifting, orchestral with modern electronic elements
- **Progression:** Building from contemplative to triumphant
- **Cultural Elements:** Subtle incorporation of global musical traditions
- **Volume:** Supporting narration without overwhelming

### Sound Effects
- **Minimal Usage:** Only to enhance key moments
- **Technology Sounds:** Subtle digital/interface sounds for tech sections
- **Transition Effects:** Smooth audio bridges between sections
- **Emphasis:** Strategic sound punctuation for key statistics
