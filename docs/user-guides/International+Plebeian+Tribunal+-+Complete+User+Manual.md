## User Authentication

### Creating Your Account

The International Plebeian Tribunal uses a secure authentication system with multiple layers of protection.

**Registration Process:**

1. **Navigate to Registration**
   - Click "Login / Register" button
   - Select "Create Account" tab
   - Fill in required information

2. **Required Information**
   - **Username**: Unique identifier (3-30 characters)
   - **Email Address**: Valid email for verification
   - **Password**: Strong password (minimum 8 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols)
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter password for verification

3. **Security Features**
   - Password strength indicator
   - Email verification required
   - Two-factor authentication available
   - Account lockout protection

### Logging In

**Standard Login:**
1. Click "Login / Register"
2. Enter username and password
3. Click "Login" button
4. Access granted to full platform

**Forgot Password:**
1. Click "Forgot Password" link
2. Enter registered email address
3. Check email for reset instructions
4. Create new password
5. Log in with new credentials

**Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):**
1. Enable 2FA in account settings
2. Scan QR code with authenticator app
3. Enter verification code during login
4. Enhanced security for sensitive operations

### Account Security

**Password Requirements:**
- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character
- Cannot contain username or email

**Security Best Practices:**
- Use unique password for this platform
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Log out from shared computers
- Monitor account activity regularly
- Report suspicious activity immediately

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Platform Questions

**Q: What is the International Plebeian Tribunal?**
A: The International Plebeian Tribunal is a global peace movement that empowers grassroots advocacy through advanced technology. We unite local chapters worldwide while maintaining democratic principles, complete transparency, and sustainable economic models through our Tribal Coin system.

**Q: How much does it cost to join?**
A: Basic membership is completely free. Chapter leaders may have access to additional premium features, and Tribal Coin transactions may have minimal network fees. All core platform features are available at no cost.

**Q: Is the platform available in my language?**
A: Yes! The platform supports 50+ languages with automatic translation services. If your language isn't available, our community translation program allows volunteers to add new languages.

**Q: How do I start a chapter in my area?**
A: Click "Start a Chapter" on the home page, complete the registration form with your chapter information, and submit required verification documents. The approval process typically takes 7-14 days.

**Q: What if there's already a chapter in my area?**
A: You can join the existing chapter or start a specialized chapter focusing on specific demographics or issues. Multiple chapters can coexist in larger metropolitan areas.

### Security and Privacy Questions

**Q: How secure is my personal information?**
A: We use military-grade encryption, multi-factor authentication, and comprehensive audit trails. All data is protected according to international privacy standards, and you control what information is shared publicly.

**Q: Can I remain anonymous while participating?**
A: Basic participation allows pseudonymous engagement. However, chapter leadership roles require identity verification for security and accountability purposes.

**Q: Who has access to my data?**
A: Only you and authorized platform administrators have access to your personal data. Chapter coordinators can see basic membership information for their chapters. All data access is logged and auditable.

**Q: How do you prevent misuse of the platform?**
A: We employ automated content moderation, community reporting systems, and human oversight. Our Seven Division Bot System includes specialized integrity and quality monitoring.

### Tribal Coin and Economic Questions

**Q: What is Tribal Coin and how does it work?**
A: Tribal Coin (TC) is our native cryptocurrency that facilitates democratic resource allocation, rewards community contributions, and enables global coordination. It operates on blockchain technology with transparent, secure transactions.

**Q: How do I earn Tribal Coins?**
A: You can earn TC through content creation, community participation, chapter leadership, skill development, and various contribution activities. The system rewards meaningful engagement and value creation.

**Q: Can I convert Tribal Coins to regular currency?**
A: TC is primarily designed for internal platform use, but exchange capabilities may be developed as the system matures. Current focus is on community value creation rather than speculative trading.

**Q: How are Tribal Coins distributed fairly?**
A: Distribution follows democratic principles with community voting on allocation priorities. The system is designed to reward contribution and participation rather than wealth accumulation.

### Chapter Management Questions

**Q: What responsibilities do chapter leaders have?**
A: Chapter leaders coordinate local activities, manage member communications, organize events, handle resource allocation, and maintain connection with the global network while ensuring local autonomy.

**Q: How much time commitment is required for chapter leadership?**
A: Time commitment varies by chapter size and activity level. Most leaders spend 5-15 hours per week on chapter activities, with automation tools reducing administrative burden.

**Q: Can chapters operate independently?**
A: Yes! Chapters maintain local autonomy while participating in the global network. You set your own priorities, activities, and focus areas within the overall peace-building mission.

**Q: What support is available for new chapters?**
A: New chapters receive onboarding training, resource allocation, mentorship from experienced chapters, access to all platform tools, and ongoing support from the global community.

### Content and Communication Questions

**Q: What types of content can I create?**
A: You can create articles, videos, reports, guides, news updates, and educational resources. All content should align with peace-building objectives and community guidelines.

**Q: How is content quality maintained?**
A: We use automated quality checks, peer review processes, community feedback systems, and editorial oversight to maintain high content standards while preserving diverse perspectives.

**Q: Can I translate content into other languages?**
A: Yes! Translation is encouraged and rewarded with Tribal Coins. We provide both automated translation tools and human translation coordination for accuracy.

**Q: How do I report inappropriate content?**
A: Use the report button on any content, contact chapter coordinators, or reach out to platform moderators directly. All reports are reviewed promptly and appropriate action is taken.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What browsers and devices are supported?**
A: The platform works on all modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and devices (desktop, tablet, mobile). We recommend keeping browsers updated for the best experience.

**Q: Can I use the platform offline?**
A: Basic content viewing is available offline through browser caching. Full functionality requires internet connection, but we're developing enhanced offline capabilities.

**Q: How do I backup my data?**
A: Your data is automatically backed up on our secure servers. You can export your content and personal data at any time through your account settings.

**Q: What happens if I forget my password?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your password via email. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

### Participation and Engagement Questions

**Q: How can I contribute if I'm not tech-savvy?**
A: The platform is designed for users of all technical levels. You can contribute through content creation, community engagement, event participation, and sharing your expertise without technical skills.

**Q: What if I disagree with a decision or policy?**
A: We encourage diverse perspectives and democratic participation. You can participate in discussions, propose alternatives, vote on issues, and engage in constructive dialogue through our governance processes.

**Q: How do I stay updated on platform developments?**
A: Subscribe to our newsletter, follow social media accounts, participate in community forums, and enable notifications in your account settings for important updates.

**Q: Can I volunteer for specific roles or projects?**
A: Absolutely! We have volunteer opportunities across all seven divisions, from content creation to technical development to community outreach. Check the volunteer section in your dashboard.
