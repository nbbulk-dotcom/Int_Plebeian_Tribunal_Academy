Constitutional Research

Our research program focuses on constitutional law, community-based justice systems, and the practical application of sovereignty principles within legal frameworks.

Constitutional Sovereignty in South Africa

Analysis of constitutional provisions supporting community-based dispute resolution and the legal foundations for plebeian tribunals within the South African context.

Status: Published 2024

Historical Precedents for Community Justice

Comparative study of community-based justice systems across cultures and time periods, from ancient Roman tribunes to modern restorative justice programs.

Status: In Progress

Access to Justice: Barriers and Solutions

Research examining systemic barriers preventing South Africans from accessing formal courts and how community tribunals can address these gaps.

Status: Published 2023

Case Documentation

Anonymized documentation of tribunal proceedings provides insights into effective dispute resolution methods and helps refine our procedures.

Policy Development

Research-driven policy recommendations for improving access to justice, strengthening constitutional protections, and integrating community tribunals into the broader legal framework.

International Collaboration

Our research contributes to the global knowledge base of the International Plebeian Tribunal Academy, with findings shared across 50+ nations working toward similar justice reform goals.
