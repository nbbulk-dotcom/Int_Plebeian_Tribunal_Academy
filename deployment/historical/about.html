Mission and Vision

The Plebeian Tribunal of South Africa exists to restore constitutional sovereignty to the people and provide an accessible, fair system of justice that operates alongside traditional courts.

Our Mission

To empower South African citizens with the knowledge, tools, and frameworks necessary to uphold their constitutional rights and seek justice through community-based tribunals that honor both written law and natural justice.

Our Vision

A South Africa where every citizen has equal access to justice, where constitutional rights are honored and protected, and where communities have the power to resolve disputes fairly and transparently without excessive cost or bureaucratic barriers.

Core Values

  • Justice: Fair and equitable treatment for all
  • Sovereignty: Recognition of inherent human rights
  • Transparency: Open operations and accountability
  • Education: Empowering citizens with constitutional knowledge
  • Community: Building networks of mutual support

What We Do

The Tribunal provides educational resources, establishes community-based dispute resolution systems, advocates for constitutional rights, and creates frameworks for decentralized justice that complement the existing legal system.
